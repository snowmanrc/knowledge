# Animal Farm

## Summary Draft 1

The novella opens on *Manor Farm*, where the human owner, *Mr. Jones*, subjects the animal to neglect and mistreatment. This cruelty grows a collective desire for freedom among the animals, which *Old Major*, an aging boar, compiles into a vision of rebellion shortly before his death. As the animals are driven past their breaking points, the animals revolt against Jones and his men, kicking them out of the farm and taking control of it.

To manage the affairs of the farm and to prove that the animals could prosper without humans, the pigs who were the intelligent of all, were put in charge of managing *Animal Farm*, which became the new name of the farm. Two pigs in particular arose as dominant forces: *Snowball*, an inventive dreamer and *Napolean*, a power-focused strategist. Jones and his men tried to take back control of the farm but the animals succeeded in winning against them again. As the pigs established admistrative control, the farm begin to prosper. The pigs established *The Seven Commandments* which acted as set of constitional rules for all animals to follow.

Snowball, being an inventive individual, painted a world of autonomy and leisure for the animals which could start by the creation of a *windmill*. All animals agreed to help Snowball in his vision with the sole exception of Napolean, who deemed the windmill as useless. As the arguments between the two grew, Napolean resorted to an underhanded tactic and exiled Snowball out of the farm with the help of his pack of young dogs, which he had previously taken from the older dogs after the revolution and trained them in private. With Snowball exiled, Napolean took complete administrative control of the farm, appointing *Squealer*, another pig, as his assistant and propagandist.

The animals were naive and gullible as Napolean's words were enough to convince them that Snowball's intentions were ill and that he was a traitor. *Boxer*, the horse who was the strongest animal on the farm, was also under the complete influence of Napolean. Following Snowball's exile, Napolean started the construction of the windmill and accredited himself with the idea. As Napolean's reign continued, the conditions of the farm actually depreciated. There was a lack of food and the animals could not remember whether they were better fed in Jones' era or Napolean's. 

Using the ongoing food issue as an excuse, the pigs started trade with the humans, going against one of their commandments. Napolean officially started his authoritarian rule by executing all animals that he deemed as traitors, which turned out to be a major turning point as the animals finally doubted Napolean's rule and intentions. *Clover*, the gentle and caring mare, also thought of how times were like before Napolean's rule and how peace was the main motive of their revolution. Before these doubts could dwell, Squealer, using his manipulative and propagandistic tactics, shut down any thought in the animals.

The pigs continued to grew in power and live a royal life while the other farm animals were used as tools of labour. One by one, the pigs kept on breaking the commandments and each time, they provided a justification that convinced the gullible animals completely. The pigs had also developed connections with the humans for the "prosperity of Animal Farm".  The pigs had complete control of *Manor Farm*, which they renamed back because that was its original name The pigs started to resemble the same humans they had once fought against. A time came, when the animals could not tell the pigs apart from the humans.

## Summary Draft 2

The novella opens on Manor Farm, owned by Mr. Jones, who subjects the farm animals to neglect and mistreatment. Old Major, a middle white boar, was a highly respected figure among the animals. One night, he calls upon all the animals and tells them about his dream where there was no man and the animals could roam the fields freely. Major tells them how their lives are miserable and laborious. He enlightens them with the idea of revolution, adopting which they could free themselves from the cruel humans and finally taste freedom. Major dies shortly after his speech but his idea is now deep engraved in the animals.

The work of teaching and organising the other animals for the revolution naturally fell on the pigs as they were considered the cleverest. Among them, two were of quite prominence: Snowball, an inventive visionary and Napolean, a power-focused strategist. The pigs continue articulating Major's ideology, calling it Animalism, and teaching the animals about revolution. The most faithful of their students were the two cart horses: Boxer and Clover. As the mistreatment by Jones and his men continues to grow, the animals finally reach their breaking point and fight back against their human owners. The men try to fend off the animals but the animals successfully expel Jones and his men out of the farm. The animals now had complete control of the farm and renamed it from Manor Farm to Animal Farm. The pigs wrote the seven commandments which were meant to be constitutional rules that every animal must follow.

The animals, under the management of the pigs, complete the harvest ahead of schedule. Boxer in particular shows immense strength and devotes himself to the benefit of Animal Farm. Snowball organizes committees and reading classes so other animals could learn to read and write like the pigs. The committees were unsuccessful to an extent as most animals could not learn language at all. So, Snowball reduces the seven commandments to just "Four legs good, two legs bad" to aid the simpler animals. During this time, it was noticed that all the milk and apples were going to the pigs. Before any thought of distrust could arise, Squealer, a brilliant charismatic talker, convinced them that as the pigs had to do all the brain work, they needed nutrients to keep them going. This was enough to convince the gullible animals.

The news of the rebellion was spread throughtout the country via pigeons sent by Snowball, which alerted the neighbouring farms, Foxwood owned by Mr. Pilkington and Pinchfield owned by Mr. Frederick. Jones, along with his allies, attempt to retake the farm from the animals in the Battle of the Cowshed but are defeated by Snowball's strategic ambush. For their acts of bravery, Snowball and Napolean are rewarded with "Animal Hero, First Class". The animals mark the day of revolution and the Battle of Cowshed as annual holidays.

Mollie, a white mare, was among the few animals who were pampered by the humans. As she was too used to being spoiled, she could not live without it and defects from the farm to pull a carriage for a human who provides her with luxuries. Snowball, being the visionary that he was, proposes to the animals that they should build a windmill to automate labour and generate electricity. Napolean opposes the idea as he deems it useless. Both present arguments but just as Snowball is about to convince the animals, Napolean unleashes his pack of nine fierce dogs he raised in secret to chase Snowball off the farm. Napolean takes the complete administrative control of the farm and abolishes the Sunday meetings where the animals normally gave their opinions.
