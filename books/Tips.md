- **Maintain Literary Present Tense:** Always narrate plot events using present-tense verbs ("Napoleon exiles", "the pigs violate") rather than past-tense verbs ("exiled", "violated").
    
- **Eliminate Informal Phrasing:** Replace colloquialisms like "starts off," "kept on breaking," and "played dirty" with academic alternatives ("opens," "systematically violate," "employed underhanded tactics").
    
- **Use Precise Vocabulary:** Match words to their correct context (e.g., physical living conditions _deteriorate_, they do not _depreciate_ like financial assets).
    
- **Fix Spelling and Typos:** Correct recurring errors like "Napolean" to "Napoleon" and check agreement between singular/plural subjects and verbs.
