## Structure of an Essay

+ Introduction (1)
	+ Hook
	+ Lead in Sentence
	+ Thesis Statement (3 point)
		+ Topic
		+ Controlling statement
		+ 3 Reasons
	+ Transitional
+ Body (3)
	+ Topic
	+ Supporting
	+ Transitional (except for the last body paragraph)
+ Conclusion (1)
	+ Restated Thesis
	+ Summary of MI
	+ Final Thoughts
