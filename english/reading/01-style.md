## Author's Style

>How the writer uses language and structure to express their thoughts and communicate ideas.`

Can fall into two categories:
1. Formal (professional, abstract)
2. Informal (casual, concrete)

> [!tip] Analogy 
> If the message is the body, the style is how the writer dresses it.

-> Model: `CF`
Casualness Filter

1. Scan all lines and look for casualness indicators.
	1. First/Second person pronouns
	2. Contractions
	3. Slangs & Idioms
	4. Sentence fragmentation (like *anyways,...* , *well,..*)
2. Even one casualness indicator leads to the passage being informal.
3. The absence of these indicators will make the passage formal.
