## Author's Purpose
`What the author wants to achieve through his writing`

All purposes end up falling into one of these categories:
+ To inform
+ To persuade
+ To entertain

There can only be one purpose.

-> Model: `CAST`
Candidate, Action, Story, Terminology

1. Pick a candidate purpose.
2. Look for call to actions or recommendations.
3. Look for story telling or humorous aspects.
4. Verify via terminology.
